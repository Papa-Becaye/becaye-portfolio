## Write to understand

Explaining a technical choice forces me to state it clearly. Often, I realise I did not really master a subject until I tried to write about it. The journal became a thinking tool before becoming a communication tool.

## What it changes in practice

- I remember decisions made on a project better.
- I get feedback from people facing the same problems.
- I see my mistakes with more perspective a few months later.

> Sharing imperfect work is more useful than presenting perfect work nobody has seen.

## Start small

You do not need a long article every week. A paragraph about a fixed bug, an architecture diagram or a lesson from an internship is enough. Consistency is what turns scattered notes into a real learning path.
